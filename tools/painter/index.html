Colours

Up to 15 colours can be on the keyboard at once. The crossed-out one switches a key off.

Keyboard

Pick a colour, then click or drag across keys. Right-click a key to pick up its colour. Every change goes to the keyboard straight away and is stored on it about a second later, so the painting stays after you unplug.

Lighting

The painting shows when the effect is "Painted (per key)". The two-colour effects use the colour and the second colour, and the picture above previews them. The same settings are in VIA's Lighting tab.

Profiles

A profile is the painting, its colours, the effect and the effect's two colours. Profiles are kept in this browser, not on the keyboard: the keyboard holds the one look you last painted or loaded. Export them to a file to back them up or to move them to another computer.
