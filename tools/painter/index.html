Not connected.

Colours

Up to 15 colours can be on the keyboard at once. The crossed-out one switches a key off.

Keyboard

Pick a colour, then click or drag across keys. Right-click a key to pick up its colour. Every change goes to the keyboard straight away and is stored on it about a second later, so the painting stays after you unplug.

Lighting

The painting shows when the effect is "Painted (per key)". The two-colour effects move between the colour and the second colour. The same settings are in VIA's Lighting tab.
